FAQ

Questions Paul hears all the time.

Straight answers on quotations, design, timescales and how a project runs. Anything else, just ask through the contact form.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Gloucestershire, the Cotswolds and the Forest of Dean. Gloucester, Cheltenham, Stroud and Cirencester are all home turf.

How much does a new kitchen cost?

Every kitchen we fit is different, so we price each job individually rather than working from a rate card. Quotations are free and no obligation. Tell us about your project through the contact form and Paul will arrange a visit, talk through what you want, and give you a clear written price.

Do you supply the kitchen or just fit it?

Both. Most clients ask us to handle everything, from design through supply and fitting. If you have already bought your units, we are happy to fit them properly.

Will I see a design before work starts?

Yes. Photorealistic CAD design is part of every kitchen and bedroom project, so you see exactly how the finished room will look before anything is ordered.

How long does a kitchen take to fit?

It depends on the size of the job and whether there is building work involved. Paul will give you an honest timescale with your quotation and keep you informed throughout. We do not start a job we cannot finish properly.

Can you manage the whole project, including other trades?

Yes. For major projects Paul manages the job end to end, bringing in trusted electricians, plumbers and plasterers he has worked with for years, so you deal with one person from start to finish.

Are you insured?

Yes. PJA Carpentry Services carries £2m public liability insurance.

Do you take on smaller jobs?

Yes. Alongside kitchens and major projects we handle bespoke carpentry and property maintenance, from fitted wardrobes and internal doors to ongoing repairs.

How do I get a quote?

Use the contact form or email Paul with a rough idea of what you want done. Paul will get back to you to arrange a free, no obligation visit and quotation.

Didn’t find your answer?

Ask Paul directly and get a free, no obligation quotation while you’re at it.
